The Canvas for Choreography

"Roam remembers so the choreographer doesn't have to."

Dance is the only major art form with no persistent memory. Roam fixes that. It holds the work so the artist can focus on creating. A mobile-first choreography app designed to help dancers and choreographers capture, organize, and refine their creative work.

What Is Roam?

1

Capture-First Entry

Record ideas instantly, organize later. One tap to record, zero setup required. No more losing brilliant moments to setup friction.

2

Session Workbench

All creation tools in one unified timeline view. Multi-track timeline with music, clips, notes, and loop regions.

3

Voice-First Notes

Pin thoughts at timecodes without breaking creative flow. Text or voice notes attached to specific moments in the music.

4

Structured Feedback

Share work with guided critique using the Liz Lerman method. Turn casual opinions into actionable feedback.

5

Formation Mapping

Visual floor marks for dancer positioning. 2D top-down view for choreographing group formations.

6

Reference Integration

Import YouTube, Bilibili, and Xiaohongshu videos directly. Mirror, slow motion, and A-B loop without leaving the app.

The Problems Roam Solves

Problem 1

Bilibili's A-B loop resets on every interaction. 20-30 minutes lost per session to manual re-scrubbing. This is the single highest time cost in active rehearsal.

Problem 2

A move spotted in a tutorial video cannot be bookmarked. It must be re-found every time. Mental overhead compounds across a session.

Problem 3

Person A proposes something, isn't sure of the exact form. Person B waits. No shared working memory. The idea is gone before evaluation.

Problem 4

Invented sections aren't attached to song structure. At assembly time, they have no home. They're left out of the final piece.

Problem 5

Alone in the studio with a song you know deeply, you freeze. No record of your own movements. No scaffolding to build from.

Problem 6

The inspiration is there, but there's no structure to hold the creative energy. No place for the half-formed thing to land before it disappears.
