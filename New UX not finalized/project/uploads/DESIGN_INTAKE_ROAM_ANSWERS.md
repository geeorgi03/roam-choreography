## What is ROAM, in one or two sentences?

**Roam is a mobile-first app for choreographers and dancers: capture video ideas fast (with or without a song), keep them in sessions or the Inbox, align clips to music and sections, tag for search, and share a view-only link to collect feedback—so scattered camera rolls and lost context become one organized workspace.**

---

## Which flow should I design?

**Recommendation (one flow, done well):** **`Home → Record → Quick-save`**

- It is **Phase 0** in `ROAM_CONSOLIDATED_PROJECT_PLAN.md` (capture-first entry) and the biggest unlock for the “ideas before music” persona.
- Alternative if workbench polish matters more: **`Session workbench → Clip → Tag`** (core repeat loop once a session exists).

**If the tool allows only one pill:** pick **Home → Record → Quick-save** unless the prototype goal is specifically the editing surface—then pick **Session workbench → Clip → Tag**.
